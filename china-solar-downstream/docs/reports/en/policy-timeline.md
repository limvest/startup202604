# Policy Timeline: China and Korea (2013–2026)

**November 2026 revision** · [Korean version (canonical)](../policy-timeline.md)

## China

| When | Policy | Downstream effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aug 2013 | CNY 0.42/kWh 20-year distributed subsidy | Start of residential installers and lessors |
| Oct 2014 | Poverty-alleviation solar | Government programmes "bring" solar to farmers |
| 31 May 2018 | "531" subsidy cut and caps | Mass exit of weak installers and P2P finance |
| Sep 2020 | 2060 carbon neutrality | Distributed PV re-accelerates |
| Jun 2021 | Whole-county rooftop pilot | Consolidation; roof-rental spreads |
| 2023–2024 | Grid-connection limits in many provinces | Residential down ~1/3 in 2024 |
| Jan 2025 | Distributed PV development rules | Defines non-individual residential; ends full-purchase for large distributed; self-consumption |
| Feb 2025 | Document 136 | Market prices + partial CfD for projects from 1 June; co-located storage mandate ended |
| Feb 2025 | State Grid "Guang e Bao" terminated | Residential lease payment flow disrupted |
| Apr 2025 | VPP guideline (No. 357) | VPPs as market players; 20GW by 2027, 50GW by 2030 |
| May 2025 | Pre-deadline rush | 92.92GW in one month; 315GW for 2025 |
| Dec 2025 | First distributed-PV REIT | Capital recycling for operating assets |
| Jan 2026 | Document 114 | Capacity payments for standalone storage |
| 2026 | Five provinces drop new C&I distributed PV from price protection; Shandong to drop non-individual residential (June 2026 or 2027, reports differ) | Distributed H1 2026 -62.6% |

## Korea

| --- | --- | --- |

| Jun 2024 | Jeju real-time, reserve and renewable bidding markets | VPP market pilot |

| 2025 | Mandatory renewables in public car parks | Built-environment solar |
| Feb 2026 | Next-generation distributed grid plan | Flexible connection, grid storage |

| 2026 | Sunlight Income Villages nationwide (2,500+ by 2030) | Village aggregation; storage funded by VPPs |
| Jul 2026 | Distribution-grid storage: 9 VPPs | 128MW storage, 182.4MW queue cleared |
| Aug 2026 | Setback caps (roads 0m, homes 200m) | Siting eased |

| 1 Jan 2027 | Contract market replaces RPS | KEPCO 20-year contracts; spot REC until 2029 |
