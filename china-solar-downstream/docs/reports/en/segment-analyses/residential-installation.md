# Segment: Residential Installation

**November 2026 revision** · [Korean (canonical)](../../segment-analyses/residential-installation.md)

China's residential growth phase is over: it peaked at 43.84GW in 2023, fell about a third in 2024, and 2025's 46GW was a rush before market pricing. Leaders now focus on managing owned assets, selling plants and going overseas.

| Phase | Years | Feature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subsidy entry | 2013–2017 | Thousands of local installers on fixed tariffs |
| 531 selection | 2018–2019 | Weak installers exit |
| Brand entry, county rollout | 2020–2022 | Appliance brands enter; roof rental spreads |
| Volume and grid limits | 2023–2024 | 2023 peak, 2024 decline |
| Market pricing | 2025– | Rush then slowdown; IPO withdrawal, take-private, overseas |

Key facts: Chint Anneng 1.6m+ plants (end-2024), 2m (Mar 2026), IPO withdrawn, tariff CNY 0.38 → 0.33/kWh; Trina Richome 1.3m+ households (~20% share); Skyworth PV 29.3GW (end-2025), 2024 revenue CNY 20.1bn, ~5% segment margin; Zhonglai 2025 net loss CNY 1.37bn.

Judgement: dealer-network reuse delivered speed, not margin; low penetration was not the constraint — grid capacity and pricing were; the remaining edge is operating owned assets. Korea: fewer detached homes (53.1% of households in apartments) and the analogous rental programme ended in 2021.
