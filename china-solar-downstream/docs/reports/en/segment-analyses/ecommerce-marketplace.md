# Segment: Marketplaces, Information and Installer SaaS

**November 2026 revision** · [Korean (canonical)](../../segment-analyses/ecommerce-marketplace.md)

No consumer solar comparison platform emerged in China because purchases came through government programmes and installer sales, not consumer search. The segment's reality is installer digitalisation. This conclusion is kept.

Phases: media/directories (2005–2015), S2B2C experiments (2016–2018), installer digitalisation (2019–), captive malls of large installers (2022–). Asset-management software may grow as lessors became core investors. Korea's distribution is more consumer-driven, but low purchase frequency and subsidy/village programmes make the opportunity smaller than the May edition claimed.
