# Segment: Grid Integration (VPP, Storage, Forecasting, Trading) — new in November 2026

**New in November 2026** · [Korean (canonical)](../../segment-analyses/grid-integration.md)

The May edition had no layer for moving dispersed resources with the grid. After Document 136 in China and surging curtailment in Korea, this is where money flows most clearly.

What it does: forecast output and prices; control output on grid instruction or shift it to storage; store and earn capacity payments; aggregate small resources into wholesale markets, PPAs and auctions.

China: VPP guideline (Apr 2025, 20GW by 2027, 50GW by 2030); Document 136 (market exposure, no storage mandate); Document 114 (capacity payments, Jan 2026); observability/control rules (Guoneng Rixin forecasting devices +104.6% in 2025).

Korea: small-scale brokerage (2018); Jeju real-time/reserve/renewable bidding (2024); semi-central dispatch (Mar 2026; sub-20MW via VPPs; Mar–May and Sep–Nov, 10:00–17:00; ~470MW registered, Haezoom 233MW, KRW 9.42/kW); distribution-grid storage (Jul 2026; 9 operators incl. VPP Lab, LG Energy Solution, KEPCO KDN, SK Eternix, HD Hyundai Electric, Gridwiz, EWP, KOMIPO, Hyundai E&C; 128MW/640MWh; 182.4MW queued solar connected); village storage funded by VPPs; contract market VPP joint bidding (2027).

Judgement: in both countries the share of operators who manage curtailment (volume) risk grows; competition is on forecast accuracy, dispatch compliance (Haezoom reported 96.19% in spring 2026), market access and storage assets.
