# Segment: Finance and Leasing

**November 2026 revision** · [Korean (canonical)](../../segment-analyses/financing-leasing.md)

Bank- and state-affiliated lessors owned residential PV at scale on low-cost capital. The edge worked only while fixed tariffs guaranteed cash flow; new investment collapsed after Document 136.

Facts: Huaxia Leasing 520k plants, ~15GW, 24 provinces (end-2024), now promoting quality certification; Yuexiu new managed capacity 4.25GW (2023) → 7.47GW (2024) → 1.44GW (2025); State Grid's "Guang e Bao" terminated (Feb 2025); Hoymiles–Huarong CNY 1bn lease tie-up ended with CNY 162m used; some localities suspended filings to curb residential leasing. Counter-signal: first C&I distributed-PV REIT (~130MW, CNY 320m, Dec 2025).

Judgement: cheap capital matters only when cash flow is predictable; money moves to structures where someone keeps operating responsibility; retail-funded long-term assets are fragile (Solarbao). Korea already tried a similar model (2013–2021); from 2027 KEPCO as long-term buyer may improve financing.
