# Segment: O&M, Monitoring and Energy Management

**November 2026 revision** · [Korean (canonical)](../../segment-analyses/om-monitoring.md)

Monitoring platforms gain users when inverters are sold, so they follow hardware. After Document 136 they are becoming VPP dispatch tools; firms whose hardware demand fell saw results fall too.

| Company | 2025 | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sungrow (parent) | Revenue CNY 89.2bn; storage overtook inverters | iSolarCloud linked to European VPP dispatch |
| Ginlong | Revenue CNY 6.95bn (+6%); Q1 2026 profit -69% | OEM platforms track hardware cycles |
| Growatt | Revenue CNY 5.23bn; storage 63.4%; AI EMS 1.2% | Software revenue small |
| Hoymiles | Revenue CNY 1.93bn; net loss CNY 161m | Module-level data did not protect results |
| Guoneng Rixin | Revenue CNY 717m (+30%); profit CNY 130m (+39%) | Forecasting/trading demand surged |
| Sigenergy | Revenue CNY 9.0bn; 99% overseas | Hardware for foreign subsidy markets |

The "hardware-SaaS flywheel" is dropped. Software value comes from grid and market links. In Korea, with Chinese inverters reported above 90%, Chinese OEM platforms likely already hold much of the device data (inference); local room lies in VPP and curtailment response.
