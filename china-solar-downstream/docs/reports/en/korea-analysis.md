# Korea Applicability Analysis

**November 2026 revision** · [Korean version (canonical)](../korea-analysis.md)

Korea resembles China's post-2025 phase rather than its 2015–2024 boom. The bottleneck is the grid, and policy has started to pay those who aggregate small resources and move them with the grid. Of the May edition's five recommendations, two had already been tried, two face changed conditions, and only one (VPP/energy management) pointed the right way.

## 1. Korea today

| Item | Status |
| --- | --- |
| New build | 3.1GW in 2025; 3.5GW forecast for 2026 |
| Fleet | 200k+ solar plants, ~32GW (June 2026) |
| Targets | 100GW renewables by 2030; solar 30.8GW → 87GW |
| Curtailment | 2 days (2023) → 26 (2024) → 82 (2025) → 59 events in H1 2026; Jan–May 2026 = 95.3% of 2025 total, Honam 51.3% |
| Equipment | Chinese modules 69.3%, cells 96.1% (2025); inverters 90%+ (reported) |
| Housing | 53.1% of households in apartments (2023); apartments 65.8% of housing stock (2025) |

## 2. Rules: opposite direction, same destination

| Item | China | Korea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Market price + partial CfD for new projects from June 2025 | KEPCO 20-year contracts replace REC spot from 2027; weights become "preferential prices" |
| Small players | Individual residential keeps more protection | Dedicated sub-1MW contract market (e.g. Sunlight Income Villages), RESCO support |
| Control | Observability/control requirements for distributed PV | Semi-central dispatch; sub-20MW via VPPs, paid for controllable capacity |
| Aggregation | VPPs recognised (Apr 2025), 50GW by 2030 | Small-scale brokerage (2018), "integrated power plant business" licence type, VPP joint bidding in contract market |
| Storage | Capacity payments for standalone storage (Jan 2026) | Distribution-grid storage round (9 VPPs, 128MW) |

China pushed price risk onto the market; Korea reduces it through KEPCO contracts. Both see rising curtailment risk and a growing role for VPPs. The May claim that KEPCO would block distributed solar weakens: KEPCO becomes the long-term buyer, which analysts say improves bankability.

## 3. Re-rating the May recommendations

| May recommendation | Verdict | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank-affiliated residential leasing | Withdrawn | Tried via the 2013–2021 rental programme; ended as subsidies expanded and certificate prices fell |
| Sigenergy-style home storage | Conditional | No home-storage subsidy or TOU signal yet; Chinese all-in-one products would likely arrive first |
| Consumer comparison marketplace | Downgraded | Relied on the 65% apartment figure (53.1% of households); no basis for market size |
| Residential REC aggregation | Withdrawn | Exists since 2018; new RECs end in 2026 |
| C&I AI energy management | Kept, sharpened | Reframed as VPP participation in curtailment, contract market and PPAs |

## 4. Open opportunities

VPP/aggregation (~470MW in semi-central dispatch, KRW 9.42/kW); distribution-grid storage (128MW connecting 182.4MW of queued solar); village and small-asset development (VPPs now fund village storage); bundled corporate PPAs (13 contracts in 2023 → 118 by May 2026).

## 5. Three lessons from China

1. Look at what survived after guarantees ended: forecasting/trading software, storage, build-and-sell.
2. Chinese equipment arrives first; localisation policy is the only lever that changes this.
3. Design consumer protection early; roof-rental models created disputes in China, and fault-repair delays were the top complaint in Korean owner interviews.

## 6. Limits

Public sources only; curtailment figures cited via an operator's summary of KPX data; inverter share is press-reported.
